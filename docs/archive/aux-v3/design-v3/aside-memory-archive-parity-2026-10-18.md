## Preserved behavior and intentional differences

- Aux naming, Geist/Geist Mono, source provenance, Space controls, model routes and account limitations remain Aux-specific. No account, activity, cost or source content is invented by production UI.
- Owner-written prose, generated document content and exact original quotes remain distinct. A legacy claim without an original quote reports the quote unavailable; it does not relabel generated synthesis as a source quote.
- Document Save/Delete retain exact generation/revision checks. Dream proposal review and Revert use the existing guarded controls. Controls retire with their original Space/window/view. Search/read and paginated activity results are fenced against refresh/Forget publication races.
- Activity displays up to 50 entries at a time. Further Dream pages are requested with `limit: 50`, deduplicated by operation ID, and published only for the current generation/request. Selection is retained across append.
- Below the Settings content container breakpoint, the tree/activity selector stacks above its detail with bounded scrolling. Explicit grid rows prevent unused stretched navigation space. The document label occupies its own narrow header line; actions remain reachable. Diffs stack to one column, and archived snippets move to a second row with ellipsis.
- Archive deletion is reviewed against the actual selected chat. After asynchronous confirmation, a replaced/deleted/retired collection item is refused rather than deleting a different revision. Escape returns focus from the menu to its ellipsis button.
